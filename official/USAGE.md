# Official GoreeCloud Logo Usage

## Status

I have approved **Unified Clean — Blue** as the official GoreeCloud platform mark. The canonical geometry is maintained in `goreecloud-logo.svg`. Derivatives in this directory preserve that geometry and exist only to support legitimate presentation contexts.

## Canonical colors

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| GoreeCloud Blue | `#3B82F6` |
| GoreeCloud Deep Blue | `#174EA6` |
| GoreeCloud Light Field | `#F7FAFF` |
| Monochrome Dark | `#111827` |
| Reversed | `#FFFFFF` |

The two approved blues define the primary full-color mark. I do not substitute application accent colors, product colors, gradients, or third-party brand colors into the official platform mark.

## Meaning

The enclosing form represents a digital environment that I own and control. Its open boundary represents independence without isolation. The central point represents the individual or family at the center of GoreeCloud. The outward path represents deliberate connection to the wider digital world on my terms.

The mark intentionally avoids literal servers, applications, network diagrams, shields, locks, or conventional cloud pictograms. GoreeCloud is defined by ownership, privacy, independence, continuity, preservation, and personal control rather than by any particular application or infrastructure implementation.

## Files

- `goreecloud-logo.svg` — canonical full-color mark on the approved light field.
- `goreecloud-mark-transparent.svg` — full-color mark for contexts requiring transparency.
- `goreecloud-mark-monochrome.svg` — single-color dark mark for constrained reproduction.
- `goreecloud-mark-reversed.svg` — white mark for sufficiently dark backgrounds.

## Clear space

I keep surrounding content away from the mark. As a baseline, the clear space on every side should be at least the diameter of the central circular opening. More space is preferred for prominent identity use.

## Minimum size

I preserve the mark at a size where the central opening and outward path remain visually distinct. For raster or screen use, I do not intentionally render the primary mark below 24 CSS pixels without first validating the result. Very small favicon derivatives may receive optical rasterization adjustments but may not change the identity geometry.

## Backgrounds

I use the canonical full-color mark on the approved light field when possible. The transparent version may be used on clean light backgrounds with sufficient contrast. The reversed mark is intended for dark backgrounds. I avoid visually busy imagery behind the mark.

## Prohibited changes

I do not stretch, skew, rotate, crop, outline, redraw, rearrange, add effects to, recolor arbitrarily, or change the relative geometry of the official mark. I do not place text or other symbols inside it. Glaze UI presentation effects may surround the mark in interfaces, but they do not alter the canonical logo artwork itself.

## Product identities

This is the GoreeCloud platform identity. Individual GoreeCloud applications, services, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Glaze UI, GoreeCloud Mesh, and other named identities may have their own approved artwork. Those assets are governed and stored in the unified `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-branding-assets` repository without modifying or masquerading as the canonical GoreeCloud platform mark.

## Source of truth

The approved SVG files in this repository are the authoritative vector sources. PNG, favicon, social-media, documentation, launcher, and other raster derivatives must be generated from approved sources here and must not become independent masters.
