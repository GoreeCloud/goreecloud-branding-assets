# GoreeCloud Wallpapers

This directory is the canonical repository namespace for GoreeCloud-themed wallpapers.

## Storage requirement

All GoreeCloud-themed wallpapers must be stored and maintained in `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-branding-assets`. Canonical wallpaper files belong under `wallpapers/` or purpose-specific subdirectories beneath it. Consumer repositories may package synchronized derivatives only when operationally required; they must not become independent sources of truth.

## Quality

Wallpapers must be high-quality and production-ready. 4K output is preferred whenever practical. Lower-resolution or device-specific derivatives may be provided when needed for compatibility, performance, packaging, or presentation, but they must preserve the approved source composition and visual quality.

## Form-factor coverage

Wallpaper sets should cover the relevant GoreeCloud surfaces and display classes, including:

- phones;
- televisions;
- desktop computers;
- tablets;
- laptops;
- ultrawide displays;
- foldable devices;
- other current or future supported form factors.

Where one composition cannot crop safely across all displays, maintain approved variants optimized for portrait, landscape, square or near-square, television, desktop, tablet, and other relevant aspect ratios.

## Brand and production requirements

Wallpaper artwork must remain consistent with current GoreeCloud and Glaze UI visual standards, preserve approved brand geometry and color treatment, avoid distortion or unintended cropping, and remain visually suitable at the dimensions for which it is published.

Superseded or rejected wallpaper artwork must not be retained as a current asset collection. Historical traceability belongs in Git history and governed change records.

See `../PRODUCTION-ASSETS.md`, `../BRAND.md`, and the authoritative GoreeCloud `Standard — Visual Assets` for broader branding and production requirements.
