# GoreeCloud Wordmark — Round 1

These wordmark and horizontal-lockup studies are **review candidates only**. The approved Unified Clean — Blue platform symbol remains unchanged and authoritative in `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`.

## Objective

I am extending the approved GoreeCloud symbol into a wordmark system that can identify GoreeCloud in headers, websites, documentation, social graphics, repositories, and other platform-wide contexts without changing the approved mark.

The wordmark must feel calm, modern, durable, and compatible with Glaze UI while remaining visually subordinate to the meaning and geometry of the approved GoreeCloud symbol.

## Candidate 01 — Balanced

`wordmark-01-balanced.svg`

A strong neutral wordmark using the full GoreeCloud name in one dark tone. This gives the symbol primary ownership of the blue identity while the name remains highly legible and restrained.

## Candidate 02 — Two Tone

`wordmark-02-two-tone.svg`

Splits the name visually into `Goree` and `Cloud`, using the approved primary blue for `Cloud`. This creates a stronger verbal connection to the approved blue identity without changing the symbol.

## Candidate 03 — Soft

`wordmark-03-soft.svg`

Uses the deep GoreeCloud blue for the full name with a subtle primary-blue baseline. This is the most Glaze-compatible presentation of the first round, but the baseline adds a secondary graphical element and therefore requires careful review.

## Candidate 04 — Spaced

`wordmark-04-spaced.svg`

Uses an uppercase, widely spaced presentation for a more institutional and enduring identity. It is intentionally less application-like and more suitable for platform-level or documentary presentation.

## Typography status

Typography in this round is exploratory and references a modern system-sans stack. It is not yet a canonical font contract. If a direction is approved, I will convert the final wordmark to deterministic vector outlines or otherwise establish an explicit approved typeface and licensing/distribution policy so the official lockup does not depend on uncontrolled font substitution.

## Approval boundary

No wordmark candidate in this directory is official until I explicitly approve it. The symbol itself is already approved and must not be changed as part of wordmark selection.
