# GoreeCloud Logo — Round 2

Status: **Approval candidates — not official artwork**

Round 1 concepts A, B, and C are rejected by the owner and must not be promoted, packaged, or represented as GoreeCloud's official platform identity.

This round deliberately removes generic cloud, server, node, shield, and network-diagram symbolism. The goal is a simpler proprietary mark that remains recognizably GoreeCloud at favicon, launcher, repository-avatar, social-avatar, documentation, and platform-branding sizes.

The candidates use rounded geometry, restrained green gradients, and uncluttered silhouettes to remain compatible with Glaze UI without turning the platform logo into an application icon.

## Candidate 01 — Refined Bars

`goreecloud-concept-01-refined-bars.svg`

The closest evolutionary direction to the existing GoreeCloud vertical-bar motif. Four rounded green columns form the primary silhouette, with a single horizontal negative-space cut creating a stronger proprietary rhythm. This candidate prioritizes continuity with the existing identity rather than introducing a new metaphor.

## Candidate 02 — Custom G

`goreecloud-concept-02-custom-g.svg`

A standalone GoreeCloud G drawn as one heavy rounded ribbon. It intentionally avoids clouds and infrastructure symbols. The shape is optimized for immediate recognition and should remain readable at small sizes.

## Candidate 03 — GC Monogram

`goreecloud-concept-03-gc-monogram.svg`

Two overlapping rounded ribbons form an interlocking G/C relationship. The lighter G carries GoreeCloud's primary green identity while the deeper green C provides visual depth. This is the most explicitly monogram-based candidate.

## Candidate 04 — Core Bars

`goreecloud-concept-04-core-bars.svg`

A simplified four-bar emblem with a quiet central negative-space core. It develops the existing vertical-bar language into a compact platform mark without introducing literal cloud imagery. This candidate is intended to feel more emblematic and less typographic than Candidate 01.

## Approval boundary

No candidate in this directory is canonical. Owner approval is required before refinement or promotion. Approval of a direction should be followed by geometry refinement, monochrome validation, compact-size raster validation, light/dark presentation review, mask-safe launcher review, wordmark development, safe-area definition, and final canonical asset placement.
