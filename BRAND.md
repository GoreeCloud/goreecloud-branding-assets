- **Document type:** Brand identity and artwork record

## Purpose

I use this record to document the approved GoreeCloud platform logo, its meaning, canonical geometry, approved color direction, usage boundary, and the selected direction for the GoreeCloud wordmark.

The GoreeCloud platform logo identifies GoreeCloud as a whole. It is not a generic application icon and must not replace the unique canonical identities required for individual GoreeCloud applications and services.

## Platform-Mark Approval Decision

I approved the **Unified Clean** direction as the official GoreeCloud logo on August 19, 2026. I also approved blue as the official color direction for this mark.

Earlier exploration rounds and alternative Signal variants are historical design concepts only. They are not approved production artwork. The canonical production source is the SVG stored at `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`.

## Wordmark Direction Decision

I selected **Two Tone — Deep Cloud** as the GoreeCloud wordmark direction on August 19, 2026.

The selected treatment pairs the approved GoreeCloud platform symbol with the name **GoreeCloud** and gives the two parts of the name a restrained hierarchy:

- **Goree** uses a dark neutral treatment.
- **Cloud** uses GoreeCloud Deep Blue `#174EA6`.
- The approved Unified Clean platform symbol remains unchanged and retains its approved blue geometry.

I selected Deep Cloud from the Two Tone refinement family because it preserves the clarity of the GoreeCloud name while using the approved deep blue to distinguish `Cloud` without introducing an unrelated color, effect, or illustration.

This decision selects the **wordmark direction**, not an independently redrawn platform symbol. The current refinement remains subject to production finalization for exact typography, optical spacing, deterministic vector construction, symbol-to-wordmark spacing, vertical alignment, small-size behavior, and light/dark presentation.

Until that production work is completed and explicitly promoted, the canonical platform artwork remains `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`. Experimental and refinement wordmark files must not supersede that canonical source merely because Deep Cloud has been selected.

## Identity Meaning

The logo is an abstract GoreeCloud mark rather than a literal illustration of a cloud, server, application, network, house, shield, or other implementation-specific object.

Its visual meaning is:

- **Outer form:** my personally controlled digital environment and the GoreeCloud space that belongs to me.
- **Open boundary:** independence without isolation. GoreeCloud remains capable of interacting with the wider digital world without surrendering ownership or control.
- **Central core:** the individual and family remain at the center of GoreeCloud rather than a provider, application, or infrastructure component.
- **Outward path:** intentional connection to external systems and the wider digital world on my terms.
- **Unified geometry:** ownership, privacy, independence, continuity, and controlled connection are treated as parts of one coherent environment.

The logo therefore represents what GoreeCloud is about rather than the particular platforms, applications, servers, or technologies used to implement it.

## Canonical Geometry

The canonical platform mark is defined by the vector paths in `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`. The SVG is the authoritative geometric source.

Raster exports, favicons, social-profile images, documentation artwork, repository artwork, and other derivatives must preserve the same underlying identity. Platform-specific adaptations may adjust rasterization, padding, masking, or presentation when required, but must not redraw or reinterpret the core mark without approval.

The wordmark must never alter the approved platform-symbol geometry. Its production vector source will be documented separately when the Deep Cloud direction is finalized.

## Planned Official Lockups

Production finalization of Deep Cloud is expected to establish controlled GoreeCloud lockups for appropriate contexts, including:

- horizontal symbol + wordmark;
- stacked symbol + wordmark;
- light-background presentation;
- dark-background/reversed presentation;
- monochrome presentation where color reproduction is unavailable;
- transparent-background vector and raster derivatives.

These lockups are presentation arrangements of the approved identity. They do not create alternate GoreeCloud platform symbols.

## Usage Boundary

I use this logo to identify the GoreeCloud ecosystem and platform as a whole.

I do not use the GoreeCloud platform logo as the generic default icon for every GoreeCloud application or service. Individual applications and services retain their own unique canonical visual identities while sharing compatible GoreeCloud and Glaze UI visual DNA.

The logo and approved lockups may be used for appropriate GoreeCloud-wide surfaces such as platform identity, organization/repository identity, public branding, documentation, ecosystem-level presentation, social identity, and other contexts where GoreeCloud itself—not one individual product—is the subject.

## Production Requirements

All official derivatives must:

- originate from an approved canonical vector source;
- preserve the approved platform-symbol geometry;
- preserve the selected Deep Cloud hierarchy when using the full-color wordmark;
- remain recognizable at their intended size;
- avoid stretching, skewing, arbitrary rotation, or structural alteration;
- avoid unapproved recoloring;
- retain adequate surrounding space and contrast;
- remain visually distinct from individual application and service icons;
- use vector artwork where practical and high-quality raster exports where raster formats are required.

## Continuing Work

The approved mark establishes the canonical GoreeCloud platform logo and blue identity direction. The selected Deep Cloud wordmark direction now establishes the basis for final wordmark production.

The next wordmark work is production refinement rather than a new conceptual round. It includes exact typography and geometry, deterministic vector construction, optical spacing, horizontal and stacked lockups, reversed and monochrome presentations, transparent derivatives, and validation at intended production sizes.

Any material change to the underlying platform-symbol geometry, identity meaning, approved blue direction, or selected Deep Cloud wordmark hierarchy requires a new explicit decision and corresponding documentation update.

## Final Decisions

I approve Unified Clean in blue as the official GoreeCloud platform logo.

I select **Two Tone — Deep Cloud** as the GoreeCloud wordmark direction, with `Goree` in a dark neutral treatment and `Cloud` in GoreeCloud Deep Blue `#174EA6`. I will promote the wordmark to canonical production artwork only after its final typography and vector construction are completed and validated.

I maintain these decisions in this repository so GoreeCloud identity can be reproduced consistently, validated, versioned, and carried across appropriate surfaces without depending on undocumented artwork or memory.
